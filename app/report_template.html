Methods, checks and limitations

Budget ID Register, Budget, Actuals and Forecasts are read. The scenario guide is excluded from all driver analysis. The generated report runs offline and sends no workbook data to a server.

  • Each account and cost center must agree with the register. Duplicate keys, unknown IDs, inconsistent groups, missing budget months, invalid dates, mixed currencies and invalid amounts stop generation.
  • Actual vs budget reconciles as volume + rate/vendor mix + unbudgeted spend + posting timing. Actual vs forecast reconciles as actual vs budget minus forecast vs budget. Forecast vs budget separates closed actual variances from remaining estimate revisions. Total actuals and budgets reconcile to source tables.
  • Volume = (net actual service units − budget units) × budget rate. Rate/vendor mix = service actual expense − net actual service units × budget rate. If budget units are zero, service expense is classified as unbudgeted.
  • Posting timing = expense posted in the selected months − expense attributed to service months in that selection. Timing within a full year can net to zero; monthly views reveal shifts.
  • Credits and reversals have signed quantities and may affect the volume component. Price, mix, credits and project scope cannot always be causally separated from these fields. Drivers describe measured differences, not confirmed business causes.
  • A zero-actual month may mean a missing invoice, unused budget or a cancelled activity. An alternate vendor is a difference from plan, not proof of an unapproved supplier.
  • This version expects the supplied 2026 schema, one budget row per ID/month, comparable billable units within each ID, and USD. Amounts are shown to cents. Variance % is unavailable for zero budget.
  • The report is a snapshot. Rerun the Python script after editing the workbook. CSV files in the report folder include every monthly ID bridge and annual summaries.
